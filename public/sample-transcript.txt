Loyalty App CRM Kickoff — 8 October 2026
Participants: Sarah (Product Owner), Marcus (Tech Lead), Priya (Scrum Master), James (Dev), Leila (Dev), Tom (QA)

Sarah: Thanks everyone for joining. Today we're kicking off the Loyalty App CRM integration. Our goal is to build a points-based loyalty system that integrates with the existing CRM so that member activity drives points automatically.

Marcus: From a technical standpoint I see three major areas. First, we need a Member Registration service — sign-up flow, profile management, tier assignment. Second, a Points Engine that listens to CRM events and awards points based on configurable rules. Third, a Rewards Catalogue where members can browse and redeem.

Priya: How big is Member Registration?

Marcus: It's XL — probably 8 points. We have identity verification, duplicate detection, the tier logic (Bronze, Silver, Gold), and we need to sync back to the CRM. I'd put confidence at 0.9.

Sarah: Points Engine is the heart of the system. Members earn points for purchases, referrals, and social actions. The rules engine needs to be configurable without a code deploy. I'd call that an XL too — 8 points — but confidence is lower, maybe 0.75, because the rules DSL is still being designed.

James: Rewards Catalogue feels like an L to me — 5 points. Browse, search, filter by tier. Redemption flow is separate.

Leila: Redemption Flow is M — 3 points. Point deduction, confirmation email, voucher generation. High confidence, 0.85.

Marcus: Important dependency: the Points Engine cannot go live until Member Registration is complete. It needs member IDs and tier data.

Tom: We should also track impediments. We don't have the CRM API credentials yet — that's blocking James and Leila's integration work. Owner is Sarah.
